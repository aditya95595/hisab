How exact age is worked out

Counting age looks simple until leap years and 28-day Februaries get involved. The correct method counts full years first, then full months, then leftover days — using each month's actual length.

That is why two calculators can disagree by a day: approximations divide total days by 365.25, while this one walks the calendar properly.

Age questions, answered

How is age in months and days calculated?

By calendar difference: full years first, then remaining full months, then remaining days — accounting for the different lengths of months and leap years.

Why does my age differ across calculators?

Some calculators divide total days by 365.25, which approximates. A calendar-correct method counts actual years, months and days, and handles leap years exactly.

Does the age calculator work for future dates?

No — a birthdate in the future is not a valid age. The calculator asks for a date on or before today.
