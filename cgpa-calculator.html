CGPA—

The ×9.5 rule, explained

CBSE's official conversion multiplies CGPA by 9.5 to get the percentage — so 8.6 CGPA becomes 81.7%. It is a board-level convention, not a universal law of mathematics.

Other boards and universities publish their own formulas, and some ask for CGPA directly. For job forms and admissions, always follow the converting rule printed by your own institution.

CGPA questions, answered

How do you convert CGPA to percentage?

Under the CBSE convention, multiply your CGPA by 9.5. For example, a CGPA of 8.6 is 8.6 × 9.5 = 81.7%.

Is the 9.5 multiplier used by all boards?

No. The ×9.5 formula is CBSE's official conversion. Other boards and universities use their own formulas — always check your institution's rule for official purposes.

How do I convert percentage back to CGPA?

Divide the percentage by 9.5 under the CBSE convention. For example, 81.7% ÷ 9.5 = 8.6 CGPA.
