FAQ

Common questions

Is Hisab free to use?

Yes. Every calculator on Hisab is free, with no accounts, no limits and no hidden charges.

Is my data uploaded anywhere?

No. All calculations happen in your browser on your own device. Nothing you type is sent to any server.

How accurate are the calculators?

They use standard published formulas. Results are estimates for planning — your bank's exact figures may differ slightly due to rounding and their own conventions.

Do I need to sign up?

No. There are no accounts on Hisab. Open a calculator and start typing.

Which calculator should I use for a home loan?

Use the EMI calculator: enter the loan amount, interest rate and tenure to see your monthly EMI, total interest and total payable.
